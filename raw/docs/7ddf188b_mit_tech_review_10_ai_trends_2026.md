### 10 AI Trends for 2026 (MIT Technology Review)

**1. Humanoid Data**
Videos of human movements are being collected en masse to train humanoid robots. From sprawling "training centers" where workers complete repetitive tasks to tele-operated bots "puppeted" by strangers overseas — a bizarre effort with no guarantee of success.

**2. LLMs+**
Large language models took the world by storm. Now everyone in AI is chasing the next big thing. The low-hanging fruit may be gone, but LLMs aren't going anywhere. There's still a lot of juice left to squeeze out of this technology.

**3. Supercharged Scams**
AI is lowering the barriers for scammers and hackers, making attempts to infiltrate targets faster, cheaper, and easier than ever. Interpol warns that scam centers across Southeast Asia are embracing inexpensive AI tools to quickly target greater numbers of victims.

**4. World Models**
AI world models — systems trained to simulate how the world works — are emerging as a key research frontier.

**5. New War Room (Military AI)**
Nations are building dedicated military AI command centers and war rooms, integrating AI into defense strategy and operations.

**6. Weaponized Deepfakes**
AI-generated deepfakes are being weaponized for disinformation, fraud, and political manipulation.

**7. Agent Orchestration**
AI agents that can autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks are becoming a major battleground for tech companies.

**8. China Open Source AI Boom**
China-spurred open source AI boom is reshaping the global AI landscape, with Chinese labs releasing increasingly capable open-weight models that challenge Western closed-source approaches.

**9. Artificial Scientists**
AI systems are being applied to accelerate scientific research — from drug discovery to materials science — acting as artificial scientists.

**10. Resistance / AI Backlash**
Growing public aversion to AI is manifesting in protests and resistance movements. People are increasingly wary of AI's role in society, jobs, and personal privacy.
